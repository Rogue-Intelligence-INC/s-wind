## Abstract

Exhaustive search for grid-scale battery sizing (117,600 configs, 7-step derivation). Objective: Minimize total cost of ownership.

## 1. Problem

**Objective**: Minimize total cost of ownership

### Parameters

| Param | Sym | Min | Max | Step | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Energy Cap | $E$ | 10.00 | 200.00 | 10.000 | MWh |

**Space**: 117,600

## 2. Derivation Chain

| Step | Desc | In | Out | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CAPEX | $E, P$ | $CAPEX = E×c_E + P×c_P$ | Investment | $/system |
| 2 | Replacement | $N, Y, life$ | $N_repl = ceil(N×Y/life)$ | Cycle count | Replacements |
| 3 | Replacement cost | $N_repl, CAPEX$ | $C_repl = N_repl × CAPEX × r$ | Ratio | Future cost |
| 4 | OPEX | $E, P$ | $OPEX = (E+P) × r_opex$ | Annual | O&M |
| 5 | Revenue | $E, P, h, prices$ | $Rev = f(E, P, h, prices)$ | Services | Ancillary |
| 6 | NPV | $CAPEX, OPEX, Rev, r$ | $NPV = -CAPEX + Σ(Rev-OPEX)/(1+r)^t$ | DCF | Net present |
| 7 | LCOS | $NPV, E_del$ | $LCOS = -NPV/E_delivered$ | Definition | $/MWh |

### Flow
```
[1] E, P → CAPEX = E×c_E + P×c_P
    ↓ [Investment]
[2] N, Y, life → N_repl = ceil(N×Y/life)
    ↓ [Cycle count]
[3] N_repl, CAPEX → C_repl = N_repl × CAPEX × r
    ↓ [Ratio]
[4] E, P → OPEX = (E+P) × r_opex
    ↓ [Annual]
[5] E, P, h, prices → Rev = f(E, P, h, prices)
    ↓ [Services]
[6] CAPEX, OPEX, Rev, r → NPV = -CAPEX + Σ(Rev-OPEX)/(1+r)^t
    ↓ [DCF]
[7] NPV, E_del → LCOS = -NPV/E_delivered
```
