## Abstract

Exhaustive search for autonomous vehicle path planning (5,760 configs, 7-step derivation). Objective: Minimize travel time, collision-free.

---

## 1. Problem

**Objective**: Minimize travel time, collision-free

### Parameters

| Param | Sym | Min | Max | Step | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Horizon | $T$ | 3.00 | 10.00 | 1.000 | s |
| Time Step | $Δt$ | 0.05 | 0.20 | 0.050 | s |
| Vel Resolution | $Δv$ | 0.50 | 2.00 | 0.500 | m/s |
| Lat Resolution | $Δy$ | 0.10 | 0.50 | 0.100 | m |
| Safety Margin | $d_s$ | 1.00 | 5.00 | 0.500 | m |

**Space**: 5,760

## 2. Derivation Chain

| Step | Desc | In | Out | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | State space | $T, Δt, Δv, Δy$ | $N = (T/Δt)(v/Δv)(y/Δy)$ | Discretization | Grid size |
| 2 | Dynamics | $x, v, a$ | $x' = v, v' = a$ | Kinematics | Double integrator |
| 3 | Cost function | $t, a, j$ | $J = w_t t + w_a∫a²dt$ | Objective | Time + comfort |
| 4 | Collision check | $x, obs$ | $d_min = min||x-x_i||$ | Distance | Closest |
| 5 | Safety constraint | $d_min, d_s$ | $d_min > d_s$ | Constraint | Buffer |
| 6 | A* search | $graph, J$ | $path = A*(start, goal)$ | Algorithm | Optimal |
| 7 | Smoothing | $path$ | $traj = smooth(path)$ | Optimization | Continuous |

### Flow
```
[1] T, Δt, Δv, Δy → N = (T/Δt)(v/Δv)(y/Δy)
    ↓ [Discretization]
[2] x, v, a → x' = v, v' = a
    ↓ [Kinematics]
[3] t, a, j → J = w_t t + w_a∫a²dt
    ↓ [Objective]
[4] x, obs → d_min = min||x-x_i||
    ↓ [Distance]
[5] d_min, d_s → d_min > d_s
    ↓ [Constraint]
[6] graph, J → path = A*(start, goal)
    ↓ [Algorithm]
[7] path → traj = smooth(path)
```
