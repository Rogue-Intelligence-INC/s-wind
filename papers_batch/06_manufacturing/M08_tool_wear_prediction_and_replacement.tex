\section{Derivation Chain}

The complete derivation proceeds through 8 sequential steps:

\subsection*{Step 1: Tool wear rate}
\textbf{Inputs:} $v_c, f$

\textbf{Formula:} $\dot{VB} = k v_c^a f^b$

\textbf{Principle:} Empirical --- mm/min

\subsection*{Step 2: Wear progression}
\textbf{Inputs:} $\dot{VB}, t$

\textbf{Formula:} $VB(t) = VB_0 + \dot{VB} \cdot t$

\textbf{Principle:} Linear --- Approximation

\subsection*{Step 3: Time to threshold}
\textbf{Inputs:} $VB_{max}, VB_0, \dot{VB}$

\textbf{Formula:} $T_{fail} = (VB_{max}-VB_0)/\dot{VB}$

\textbf{Principle:} Definition --- Tool life

\subsection*{Step 4: Tool changes per shift}
\textbf{Inputs:} $T_{shift}, T_L$

\textbf{Formula:} $N_c = T_{shift}/T_L$

\textbf{Principle:} Expected --- Change count

\subsection*{Step 5: Change time cost}
\textbf{Inputs:} $N_c, t_c$

\textbf{Formula:} $C_{tc} = N_c \times t_c \times r$

\textbf{Principle:} Downtime --- Lost production

\subsection*{Step 6: Tool cost}
\textbf{Inputs:} $N_c, C_t$

\textbf{Formula:} $C_{tool} = N_c \times C_t$

\textbf{Principle:} Direct --- Purchase

\subsection*{Step 7: Quality cost}
\textbf{Inputs:} $VB, Q$

\textbf{Formula:} $C_Q = k_Q (VB/VB_{max})^2$

\textbf{Principle:} Quadratic --- Degradation

\subsection*{Step 8: Total cost}
\textbf{Inputs:} $C_{tc}, C_{tool}, C_Q$

\textbf{Formula:} $C_{tot} = C_{tc} + C_{tool} + C_Q$

\textbf{Principle:} Sum --- Minimize
