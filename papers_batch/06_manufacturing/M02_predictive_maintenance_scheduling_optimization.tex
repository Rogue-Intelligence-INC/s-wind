\section{Derivation Chain}

The complete derivation proceeds through 9 sequential steps:

\subsection*{Step 1: Failure rate}
\textbf{Inputs:} $t, \beta, \eta$

\textbf{Formula:} $\lambda(t) = (\beta/\eta)(t/\eta)^{\beta-1}$

\textbf{Principle:} Weibull --- Increasing hazard

\subsection*{Step 2: Reliability}
\textbf{Inputs:} $\lambda, T_i$

\textbf{Formula:} $R(T_i) = e^{-(T_i/\eta)^\beta}$

\textbf{Principle:} Survival function --- Probability

\subsection*{Step 3: Detection probability}
\textbf{Inputs:} $\theta, \epsilon$

\textbf{Formula:} $P_d = \Phi((\theta-0.5)/\epsilon)$

\textbf{Principle:} Normal CDF --- Sensor accuracy

\subsection*{Step 4: Preventive cost}
\textbf{Inputs:} $C_{pm}, T_i$

\textbf{Formula:} $C_p = C_{pm} \times (8760/T_i)$

\textbf{Principle:} Annual --- Scheduled

\subsection*{Step 5: Corrective cost}
\textbf{Inputs:} $C_{cm}, \lambda$

\textbf{Formula:} $C_c = C_{cm} \times (1-R) \times P_d$

\textbf{Principle:} Expected --- Failures

\subsection*{Step 6: Downtime cost}
\textbf{Inputs:} $C_d, MTTR, C$

\textbf{Formula:} $C_{dt} = C_d \times MTTR/C$

\textbf{Principle:} Crew effect --- Lost production

\subsection*{Step 7: Inventory cost}
\textbf{Inputs:} $S, C_h$

\textbf{Formula:} $C_{inv} = S \times C_h$

\textbf{Principle:} Holding --- Annual

\subsection*{Step 8: Total cost}
\textbf{Inputs:} $C_p, C_c, C_{dt}, C_{inv}$

\textbf{Formula:} $C_{tot} = C_p + C_c + C_{dt} + C_{inv}$

\textbf{Principle:} Summation --- Minimize

\subsection*{Step 9: Availability}
\textbf{Inputs:} $MTBF, MTTR$

\textbf{Formula:} $A = MTBF/(MTBF+MTTR)$

\textbf{Principle:} Definition --- Constraint > 0.99
