\section{Derivation Chain}

The complete derivation proceeds through 8 sequential steps:

\subsection*{Step 1: Total work content}
\textbf{Inputs:} $tasks$

\textbf{Formula:} $W = \sum_i t_i$

\textbf{Principle:} Sum --- All task times

\subsection*{Step 2: Minimum stations}
\textbf{Inputs:} $W, C$

\textbf{Formula:} $n_{min} = \lceil W/C \rceil$

\textbf{Principle:} Lower bound --- Theoretical

\subsection*{Step 3: Actual station time}
\textbf{Inputs:} $assigned, S$

\textbf{Formula:} $T_j = \sum_{i \in S_j} t_i / S$

\textbf{Principle:} Skill adjusted --- Per station

\subsection*{Step 4: Line efficiency}
\textbf{Inputs:} $W, n, C$

\textbf{Formula:} $E = W/(n \times C)$

\textbf{Principle:} Definition --- Utilization

\subsection*{Step 5: Balance delay}
\textbf{Inputs:} $E$

\textbf{Formula:} $BD = 1 - E$

\textbf{Principle:} Complement --- Idle time

\subsection*{Step 6: Smoothness index}
\textbf{Inputs:} $T_j, C$

\textbf{Formula:} $SI = \sqrt{\sum_j (C-T_j)^2/n}$

\textbf{Principle:} Std dev --- Variation

\subsection*{Step 7: Throughput}
\textbf{Inputs:} $C, P$

\textbf{Formula:} $TH = P/C$

\textbf{Principle:} Definition --- Units/second

\subsection*{Step 8: WIP}
\textbf{Inputs:} $n, B$

\textbf{Formula:} $WIP = n + B(n-1)$

\textbf{Principle:} Little's law --- Inventory
