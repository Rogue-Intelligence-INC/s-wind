\section{Derivation Chain}

The complete derivation proceeds through 9 sequential steps:

\subsection*{Step 1: Worst-case stack}
\textbf{Inputs:} $T_i$

\textbf{Formula:} $T_{WC} = \sum_i |T_i|$

\textbf{Principle:} Arithmetic --- Maximum

\subsection*{Step 2: RSS stack}
\textbf{Inputs:} $T_i$

\textbf{Formula:} $T_{RSS} = \sqrt{\sum_i T_i^2}$

\textbf{Principle:} Root sum square --- Statistical

\subsection*{Step 3: Standard deviation}
\textbf{Inputs:} $T_i, C_p$

\textbf{Formula:} $\sigma_i = T_i/(3C_p)$

\textbf{Principle:} Definition --- Process spread

\subsection*{Step 4: Assembly variation}
\textbf{Inputs:} $\sigma_i$

\textbf{Formula:} $\sigma_A = \sqrt{\sum_i \sigma_i^2}$

\textbf{Principle:} RSS --- Combined

\subsection*{Step 5: Fit probability}
\textbf{Inputs:} $c, \sigma_A$

\textbf{Formula:} $P = \Phi(c/\sigma_A)$

\textbf{Principle:} Normal CDF --- Constraint

\subsection*{Step 6: Tolerance cost}
\textbf{Inputs:} $T_i$

\textbf{Formula:} $C_i = k/T_i^2$

\textbf{Principle:} Empirical --- Tighter = costlier

\subsection*{Step 7: Total tolerance cost}
\textbf{Inputs:} $C_i$

\textbf{Formula:} $C_{tol} = \sum_i C_i$

\textbf{Principle:} Sum --- Manufacturing

\subsection*{Step 8: Inspection cost}
\textbf{Inputs:} $U, n$

\textbf{Formula:} $C_{insp} = k_m n / U$

\textbf{Principle:} Precision --- Measurement

\subsection*{Step 9: Total cost}
\textbf{Inputs:} $C_{tol}, C_{insp}$

\textbf{Formula:} $C_{tot} = C_{tol} + C_{insp}$

\textbf{Principle:} Sum --- Minimize
