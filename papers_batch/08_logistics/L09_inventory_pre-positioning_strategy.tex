\section{Knowledge Base}
\textbf{Economic Order Quantity} (EOQ): $Q^* = \sqrt{2DS/H}$ --- Harris, 1913

\textbf{Safety Stock} (SAFETY_STOCK): $SS = z_{\alpha} \sigma_{LT}$ --- Inventory Theory


\section{Derivation Chain}
\textbf{Step 1}: Lead time demand --- Inputs: $\mu, L$, Formula: $\mu_{LT} = \mu \times L$, Principle: Expected (Units)


\textbf{Step 2}: LT demand std --- Inputs: $\sigma, L$, Formula: $\sigma_{LT} = \sigma \sqrt{L}$, Principle: Root L (Units)


\textbf{Step 3}: Safety stock --- Inputs: $k, \sigma_{LT}$, Formula: $SS = k \sigma_{LT}$, Principle: Buffer (Units)


\textbf{Step 4}: Reorder point --- Inputs: $\mu_{LT}, SS$, Formula: $ROP = \mu_{LT} + SS$, Principle: Trigger (Units)


\textbf{Step 5}: Cycle stock --- Inputs: $EOQ$, Formula: $CS = Q/2$, Principle: Average (Units)


\textbf{Step 6}: Holding cost --- Inputs: $CS, SS, h$, Formula: $C_h = (CS + SS) \times h$, Principle: Annual ($)


\textbf{Step 7}: Stockout probability --- Inputs: $k$, Formula: $P_{so} = 1 - \Phi(k)$, Principle: Normal CDF (Per cycle)


\textbf{Step 8}: Total cost --- Inputs: $C_h, C_{so}$, Formula: $TC = C_h + C_{so} P_{so} \mu/Q$, Principle: Expected (Minimize)
