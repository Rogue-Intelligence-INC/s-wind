\section{Knowledge Base}
\textbf{Price Elasticity} (ELASTICITY): $\epsilon = (dQ/Q)/(dp/p)$ --- Economics

\textbf{Revenue Management} (REVENUE_MGMT): $\max E[R(p)]$ --- Talluri & van Ryzin


\section{Derivation Chain}
\textbf{Step 1}: Demand function --- Inputs: $p, \epsilon$, Formula: $D(p) = D_0 (p/p_0)^\epsilon$, Principle: Isoelastic (Units)


\textbf{Step 2}: Price optimization --- Inputs: $D, \epsilon$, Formula: $p^* = MC \epsilon/(\epsilon + 1)$, Principle: Monopoly (Optimal)


\textbf{Step 3}: Capacity utilization --- Inputs: $D, C$, Formula: $\rho = D(p)/C$, Principle: Ratio (Fill rate)


\textbf{Step 4}: Surge pricing --- Inputs: $\rho$, Formula: $s = 1 + (s_{max}-1)\max(0, (\rho-0.8)/0.2)$, Principle: Trigger (Multiplier)


\textbf{Step 5}: Revenue --- Inputs: $p, s, D$, Formula: $R = p \times s \times D(p \times s)$, Principle: Product ($)


\textbf{Step 6}: Lost sales --- Inputs: $D, C$, Formula: $L = \max(0, D - C)$, Principle: Overflow (Units)


\textbf{Step 7}: Opportunity cost --- Inputs: $L, margin$, Formula: $C_o = L \times margin$, Principle: Lost revenue ($)


\textbf{Step 8}: Net revenue --- Inputs: $R, C_o$, Formula: $R_{net} = R - C_o$, Principle: Objective (Maximize)
