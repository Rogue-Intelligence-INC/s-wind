\section{Derivation Chain}
\textbf{Step 1}: Inbound capacity --- Inputs: $D_i, t_u$, Formula: $C_i = D_i \times 60/t_u$, Principle: trailers/h (Throughput)


\textbf{Step 2}: Outbound capacity --- Inputs: $D_o, t_l$, Formula: $C_o = D_o \times 60/t_l$, Principle: trailers/h (Throughput)


\textbf{Step 3}: Bottleneck --- Inputs: $C_i, C_o$, Formula: $C = \min(C_i, C_o)$, Principle: Constraint (System)


\textbf{Step 4}: Wave time --- Inputs: $N_t, C$, Formula: $T_w = N_t/C$, Principle: hours (Per wave)


\textbf{Step 5}: Internal transfer --- Inputs: $pallets$, Formula: $T_t = \sum d_{ij}/v \times n_{ij}$, Principle: Travel (hours)


\textbf{Step 6}: Staging delay --- Inputs: $buffer$, Formula: $T_s = E[queue wait]$, Principle: Queueing (hours)


\textbf{Step 7}: Makespan --- Inputs: $T_w, T_t, T_s$, Formula: $M = T_w + T_t + T_s$, Principle: Sum (Minimize)


\textbf{Step 8}: Door utilization --- Inputs: $actual, capacity$, Formula: $U = actual/capacity$, Principle: Ratio (Metric)
