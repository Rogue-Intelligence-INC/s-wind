\section{Knowledge Base}
\textbf{Facility Location} (FACILITY): $\min \sum f_i y_i + \sum c_{ij} x_{ij}$ --- Weber, 1909

\textbf{Square Root Law} (SQUARE_ROOT): $SS = k\sqrt{n}$ --- Inventory Theory


\section{Derivation Chain}
\textbf{Step 1}: Demand coverage --- Inputs: $locations, r$, Formula: $cov = \sum_j \mathbf{1}(d(j, nearest) \leq r) / N$, Principle: Fraction (% served)


\textbf{Step 2}: Average distance --- Inputs: $assignments$, Formula: $\bar{d} = \sum_j d(j, assigned_i) / N$, Principle: Mean (To MFC)


\textbf{Step 3}: Delivery time --- Inputs: $\bar{d}, v$, Formula: $T_d = \bar{d}/v + t_{pick}$, Principle: Sum (Total)


\textbf{Step 4}: Pick time --- Inputs: $S, A$, Formula: $t_{pick} = k \sqrt{S} (1-A)$, Principle: Square root (Walking)


\textbf{Step 5}: Throughput --- Inputs: $C, A$, Formula: $Q = C \times (1 + 0.5A)$, Principle: Automation boost (Orders/day)


\textbf{Step 6}: Utilization --- Inputs: $demand, Q$, Formula: $\rho = D/Q$, Principle: Ratio (Load factor)


\textbf{Step 7}: Investment --- Inputs: $n, A, S$, Formula: $I = n(I_0 + I_A A + I_S S)$, Principle: Linear (CAPEX)


\textbf{Step 8}: Objective --- Inputs: $T_d, cov$, Formula: $Z = T_d + \beta(1-cov)$, Principle: Penalized (Minimize)
