\section{Derivation Chain}
\textbf{Step 1}: Manhattan distance --- Inputs: $p1, p2$, Formula: $d = |x_1-x_2| + |y_1-y_2|$, Principle: Grid (meters)


\textbf{Step 2}: Travel time --- Inputs: $d, v$, Formula: $t = d/v_{max}$, Principle: No obstacles (seconds)


\textbf{Step 3}: Collision check --- Inputs: $paths$, Formula: $C = \{(i,j): path_i \cap path_j \neq \emptyset\}$, Principle: Intersection (Conflicts)


\textbf{Step 4}: Wait penalty --- Inputs: $|C|, t_w$, Formula: $T_w = |C| \times t_w / 2$, Principle: Average (seconds)


\textbf{Step 5}: Throughput --- Inputs: $N_r, cycle$, Formula: $Q = N_r \times 3600 / t_{cycle}$, Principle: Hourly (picks/h)


\textbf{Step 6}: Congestion factor --- Inputs: $N_r, area$, Formula: $\gamma = N_r / (area/r_{min}^2)$, Principle: Density (Ratio)


\textbf{Step 7}: Effective speed --- Inputs: $v, \gamma$, Formula: $v_{eff} = v_{max} (1 - \gamma)$, Principle: Degradation (m/s)


\textbf{Step 8}: Total time --- Inputs: $d, v_{eff}, T_w$, Formula: $T = d/v_{eff} + T_w$, Principle: Sum (Minimize)
