\section{Knowledge Base}
\textbf{Traveling Salesman} (TSP): $\min \sum_{ij} c_{ij} x_{ij}$ --- Dantzig, 1954

\textbf{Vehicle Routing Problem} (VRP): $\min \sum_k D_k$ --- Dantzig & Ramser, 1959


\section{Derivation Chain}
\textbf{Step 1}: Travel distance --- Inputs: $route$, Formula: $D = \sum_{i} d(i, i+1)$, Principle: Sum (Total distance)


\textbf{Step 2}: Travel time --- Inputs: $D, v$, Formula: $T_{travel} = D/v$, Principle: Definition (Hours)


\textbf{Step 3}: Service time --- Inputs: $n, s$, Formula: $T_{service} = n \times s/60$, Principle: Sum (Hours)


\textbf{Step 4}: Total time --- Inputs: $T$, Formula: $T_{total} = T_{travel} + T_{service}$, Principle: Sum (Route duration)


\textbf{Step 5}: Arrival time --- Inputs: $t_0, route$, Formula: $a_i = t_0 + \sum_{j<i}(t_{j,j+1} + s_j)$, Principle: Cumulative (At stop i)


\textbf{Step 6}: Time window violation --- Inputs: $a_i, e_i, l_i$, Formula: $v_i = \max(0, e_i - a_i) + \max(0, a_i - l_i)$, Principle: Penalty (Early/late)


\textbf{Step 7}: Capacity utilization --- Inputs: $q, Q$, Formula: $\rho = \sum q_i / Q$, Principle: Ratio (Fill rate)


\textbf{Step 8}: Objective --- Inputs: $D, v_i$, Formula: $Z = D + \alpha \sum v_i$, Principle: Weighted (Minimize)
