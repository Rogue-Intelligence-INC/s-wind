\section{Theoretical Foundation}
\textbf{Feed Conversion Ratio} (FCR): $FCR = Feed_{input}/Biomass_{gain}$ [Aquaculture]

\textbf{Total Ammonia Nitrogen} (TAN): $TAN = NH_3 + NH_4^+$ [Water chemistry]


\section{Complete Derivation Chain}
\textbf{Step 1}: Daily feed --- $FR, BW$ $\Rightarrow$ $F = FR \times BW / 100$ (Definition: kg/day)


\textbf{Step 2}: Protein intake --- $F, CP$ $\Rightarrow$ $PI = F \times CP/100$ (Product: kg/day)


\textbf{Step 3}: Growth rate --- $PI, \eta_p$ $\Rightarrow$ $G = PI \times \eta_p$ (Conversion: kg/day)


\textbf{Step 4}: FCR --- $F, G$ $\Rightarrow$ $FCR = F/G$ (Definition: kg/kg)


\textbf{Step 5}: Ammonia production --- $PI$ $\Rightarrow$ $NH_3 = PI \times 0.092$ (Catabolism: kg/day)


\textbf{Step 6}: TAN concentration --- $NH_3, V, WE$ $\Rightarrow$ $TAN = NH_3 / (V \times WE/100)$ (Dilution: mg/L)


\textbf{Step 7}: Dissolved O2 --- $respiration, aeration$ $\Rightarrow$ $DO = DO_{sat} - R/k_{La}$ (Balance: mg/L)


\textbf{Step 8}: Objective --- $FCR, constraints$ $\Rightarrow$ $Z = 1/FCR$ (Maximize growth efficiency: )
