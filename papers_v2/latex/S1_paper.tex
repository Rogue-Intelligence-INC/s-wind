\begin{abstract}
We present a systematic exhaustive search methodology for optimization of targeted poverty alleviation strategies. Our framework evaluates 75,411 discrete parameter combinations. The optimization objective is to maximize poverty reduction rate per unit investment. We provide a complete derivation chain tracing each calculation from first principles to final results, ensuring reproducibility.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords:} poverty alleviation, social policy, optimization, development economics

\section{Introduction}

The social policy domain requires systematic optimization approaches. This paper presents exhaustive parameter search with complete derivation chains.
