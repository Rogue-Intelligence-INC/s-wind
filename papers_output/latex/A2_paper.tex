\begin{abstract}
We present a systematic exhaustive search methodology for optimal aerodynamic shape design for electric vehicles. Our framework evaluates 290,594,304 discrete parameter combinations across 7 design variables. The optimization objective is to minimize drag coefficient subject to volumetric efficiency > 0.7. Through rigorous constraint verification, we identify the optimal configuration that satisfies all declared constraints.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords:} electric vehicle, aerodynamic optimization, drag coefficient, vehicle design

\section{Introduction}

The automotive engineering domain presents significant optimization challenges. This paper presents a systematic approach through exhaustive parameter space search.
