\begin{abstract}
We present a systematic exhaustive search methodology for optimization of targeted poverty alleviation strategies. Our framework evaluates 754,110 discrete parameter combinations across 6 design variables. The optimization objective is to maximize poverty reduction rate per unit investment. Through rigorous constraint verification, we identify the optimal configuration that satisfies all declared constraints.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords:} poverty alleviation, social policy, optimization, development economics

\section{Introduction}

The social policy domain presents significant optimization challenges. This paper presents a systematic approach through exhaustive parameter space search.
