\begin{abstract}
We present a systematic exhaustive search methodology for universal basic income parameter optimization. Our framework evaluates 830,544 discrete parameter combinations across 6 design variables. The optimization objective is to maximize poverty elimination rate subject to fiscal sustainability. Through rigorous constraint verification, we identify the optimal configuration that satisfies all declared constraints.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords:} universal basic income, UBI, poverty, fiscal policy, welfare economics

The social policy domain presents significant optimization challenges. This paper presents a systematic approach through exhaustive parameter space search.
