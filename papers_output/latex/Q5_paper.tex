\begin{abstract}
We present a systematic exhaustive search methodology for optimal pathway to carbon neutrality. Our framework evaluates 17,978,688 discrete parameter combinations across 6 design variables. The optimization objective is to minimize transition cost subject to net-zero by 2050. Through rigorous constraint verification, we identify the optimal configuration that satisfies all declared constraints.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords:} carbon neutrality, climate change, energy transition, optimization

\section{Introduction}

The environmental science domain presents significant optimization challenges. This paper presents a systematic approach through exhaustive parameter space search.
