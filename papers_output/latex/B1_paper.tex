\begin{abstract}
We present a systematic exhaustive search methodology for cost optimization for large language model inference. Our framework evaluates 20,971,520 discrete parameter combinations across 6 design variables. The optimization objective is to minimize latency × power / throughput. Through rigorous constraint verification, we identify the optimal configuration that satisfies all declared constraints.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords:} LLM inference, optimization, throughput, latency, GPU

The ai infrastructure domain presents significant optimization challenges. This paper presents a systematic approach through exhaustive parameter space search.
