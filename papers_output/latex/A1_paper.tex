\begin{abstract}
We present a systematic exhaustive search methodology for optimal structural design for 30-story high-rise buildings. Our framework evaluates 673,920 discrete parameter combinations across 6 design variables. The optimization objective is to minimize steel consumption subject to safety factor > 1.5. Through rigorous constraint verification, we identify the optimal configuration that satisfies all declared constraints.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords:} high-rise building, structural optimization, steel consumption, seismic design

\section{Introduction}

The civil engineering domain presents significant optimization challenges. This paper presents a systematic approach through exhaustive parameter space search.
