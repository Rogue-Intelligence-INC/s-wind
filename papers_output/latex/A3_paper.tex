\begin{abstract}
We present a systematic exhaustive search methodology for next-generation wifi 7/8 parameter optimization. Our framework evaluates 87,048,192 discrete parameter combinations across 6 design variables. The optimization objective is to maximize throughput × coverage / power consumption. Through rigorous constraint verification, we identify the optimal configuration that satisfies all declared constraints.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords:} WiFi 7, WiFi 8, OFDMA, MIMO, wireless optimization

The wireless communications domain presents significant optimization challenges. This paper presents a systematic approach through exhaustive parameter space search.
