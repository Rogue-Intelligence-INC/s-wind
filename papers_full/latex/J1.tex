Exhaustive search of 14,700 configurations for hospital operating room scheduling optimization. Complete 7-step derivation chain.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem}
\textbf{Objective}: Maximize utilization subject to overtime < 10\%

\section{Derivation Chain}
\begin{longtable}{|c|p{2cm}|p{2cm}|p{3cm}|p{2cm}|p{3cm}|}
\hline
Step & Desc & In & Out & Rule & Why\\\hline\endhead
1 & Available time & $B, n_OR$ & $T_avail = B × n_OR × 60$ & Definition & Total OR-minutes\\\hline
2 & Case duration & $case_type$ & $μ, σ ~ LogNormal$ & Statistics & Historical data\\\hline
3 & Scheduled time & $cases, μ$ & $T_sched = Σ (μ_i + τ_buf)$ & Sum & With buffer\\\hline
4 & Utilization & $T_sched, T_avail$ & $U = T_sched / T_avail$ & Definition & Efficiency\\\hline
5 & Overtime probability & $T, σ, B$ & $P(OT) = 1 - Φ((B-T)/σ)$ & Normal & Tail probability\\\hline
6 & Urgency weighting & $cases, w$ & $Score = Σ w_i × priority_i$ & Objective & Weighted sum\\\hline
7 & Objective & $U, P(OT), Score$ & $max U × Score - λ × P(OT)$ & Trade-off & Multi-objective\\\hline
\end{longtable}
