## 📝 Paper Summary

### Title
**Structure-Free Theorems for Fundamental Physical Constants: Automated Algebraic Exploration with Exact Rational Arithmetic**

### Abstract
We introduce Structure-Free Theorems as a formal framework for documenting negative results in automated mathematical exploration. Using the S-wind system with Extended Precision Rational (EPR) arithmetic, we analyze the decimal digit sequences of 10 fundamental physical constants. Our exhaustive algebraic exploration yields one positive discovery: **Planck temperature digits exhibit a quadratic structure** f(n) = 2n²/9 − 2n/3 + 7/5 with DoF = 6 and verified OOS extrapolation. For the remaining 9 constants, we prove Structure-Free Theorems.
